Privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Clutchly is an app for reptile keepers and breeders to record their animals, feeding, sheds, weights and breeding. It is operated by Clutchly (ABN 74 123 034 998), Sippy Downs QLD 4556, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, where it is stored, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your email, the records you enter, your photos, and (if you turn notifications on) a device token.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • Your collection is private to your account. Other users cannot see it.
  • You can download all your data, and delete your account and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or any information from other apps. We do not knowingly collect information about children; Clutchly is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Where your information is stored, and who helps us

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process information only on our instructions:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage). Your account and records are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Expo (push notification delivery, United States) receives your device token and the text of your notifications, only if you turn notifications on.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management, United States) receives an anonymous account identifier and your purchase records from Apple or Google.
  • Apple and Google process payments for subscriptions under their own privacy policies.
  • Resend (email delivery, Japan) sends account emails such as password resets and sign-up confirmations. It receives your email address and the content of those emails.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia (APP 8). We choose providers with strong security practices and take reasonable steps to ensure they handle your information consistently with the APPs.

If you contact us through the app's Contact support form, we keep your message, the email address you give for our reply, your account (if you were signed in) and basic app details (app version, phone or browser type, free or Pro, and how many animals you have, but nothing about the animals themselves), so we can answer and fix problems. The message is emailed to us through Resend.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only disclose it to the providers above, or where the law requires it.

If you hand an animal over to its buyer, the buyer asks for it through the link and you approve it. When you approve, a copy of that animal's records (its details, history, genes and photos, and your notes only if you choose to include them) goes to the buyer, along with the name you sign it with. Handovers also swap wildlife licence details (full name, licence number and address, plus state, class, expiry and phone if saved), because buyers and sellers need them for their records: the buyer's go to the seller when they ask, and the seller's go to the buyer when the seller approves. If the seller declines or the buyer withdraws, the buyer's details are removed from the request. Both people keep the record of a completed handover even if one of them later deletes the animal or their account. Your email address and other animals are not shared. Until a link is used or expires, anyone with it can see the animal's name, species, sex, hatch date, genes and how many records and photos it has.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each account can only read and change its own records and photos. Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of an eligible data breach we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and records are kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your records, photos and device tokens are deleted straight away (photo files are removed from storage within an hour). Copies may remain in our provider's encrypted backups for up to 7 days before they are overwritten.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as their laws and policies require.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and download: Account > Download my data gives you a copy of everything you have entered, in a standard file format.
  • Correction: you can edit any record in the app.
  • Deletion: Account > Delete account permanently deletes your account and all its data. You can also email support@eternalreptiles.com.au from your account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Account > Notifications or in your phone's settings.

Questions and complaints

Contact us at support@eternalreptiles.com.au or Sippy Downs QLD 4556. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
