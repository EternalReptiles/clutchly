Common questions

Getting started

How do I add my animals?

On the Animals tab tap + Add for one animal, or Bulk add to paste a list or import a CSV file (name, species, sex, and optionally hatch date and last feed date).

How are feeds worked out?

Each animal is due a feed a set number of days after its last one: your routine for that species on the Season tab, or the animal's own number if you set one on its page. Without a routine, females are fed every 7 days and males every 21. Feeding pauses on the Season tab stop feeds being due. No feeds are due while an animal is In shed (until you log the shed), or while a pairing has no Male removed date (for the female and the male).

What's on the Overview tab?

What's due now, the next 7 days (feeds, expected lays and hatches, season dates), animals that need a look (refusals, dates that passed with nothing recorded) and the last 7 days.

Can two animals have the same ID?

No. Each animal's ID must be different: if you type one that's already in your collection, the app says This ID already exists and won't save until you choose another. Bulk add skips rows with an ID you already have. If a handover brings in an animal with an ID you already use, its copy gets a number added, like ER001 (2).

Feeding, sheds and weights

How do I log a feed, shed or weight?

Open the animal, pick the date, then tap Fed, Refused, Not offered (you skipped it on purpose: the schedule moves on, but it isn't counted as a feed or a refusal), In shed (feeds pause until the shed), Shed or Weight (enter grams first). Tap an entry in Recent or Full history to change or delete it.

Can I feed a whole rack at once?

Yes. On Overview tap Feed all to open everything that's due already marked Fed, or tap Feed on the Animals tab. Set the date and food once, change any animal to Refused, tap again to leave one out, then Save.

Breeding and incubation

How do I track a pairing?

On the Pairings tab tap + New pairing, pick the female and male (or tap Scan labels and scan both labels, in either order), then log locks and dates (ovulation, pre-lay shed, eggs laid, hatched) as they happen. The pairing shows the next expected date, worked out from typical figures for the species. Each choice shows the animal's species and genes. While a pairing has no Male removed date, feeds pause for both animals.

How do I log incubation?

Once a pairing has an eggs-laid date, its page shows Incubation: set the incubator and target temperature, then add readings (temperature, humidity, good eggs and a note). Readings more than 1 °C from the target show in red.

How do I add the hatchlings?

When the pairing has a hatch date, tap Create hatchlings, enter the clutch size and how many to add, and choose an ID pattern. They're added as animals linked to both parents.

How does the morph calculator work?

Record each animal's genes on its page (Edit genes). The calculator on every pairing (and Pairings > Morph calculator) shows the odds for each baby. It assumes genes are inherited independently; real clutches vary.

Labels and scanning

How do I print QR labels?

On the Animals tab tap Labels (or Print label on an animal), tick the animals and choose a size: A4 sticker sheets of 21 (63.5 × 38.1 mm, e.g. Avery L7160) or 14 (99.1 × 38.1 mm, e.g. L7163), or a label printer. Print at 100% (Actual size), not Fit to page. On a part-used sheet, enter how many labels are already gone.

Using the app from your iPhone home screen? Tapping Print opens the labels in Safari, which shows the print screen (or tap Print at the top of the page).

How do I scan a label?

Tap Scan on the Animals tab and point the camera at the label: the animal opens. Your phone's Camera app also works: it opens the animal in the web app.

Selling and buying animals

How do I send an animal's records to its buyer?

On the animal's page tap Make a handover link and send the link to your buyer by text or private message. They open it and ask for the animal; you get a card on Overview and on the animal's page with their name, wildlife licence and address. Tap Approve and they get their own copy with its history, genes and photos, your licence details go to them, and your animal is marked Sold. Tap Decline if it isn't your buyer.

I've been sent a handover link. What do I do?

Open it, sign in (or create a free account), add your wildlife licence details if asked, and tap Ask to add to my collection. When the seller approves, the animal appears in your Animals. You can also type the code on the new-animal screen or Bulk add.

Why do I need to add my wildlife licence?

Buyers and sellers need each other's full name, licence number and address for their records. Save them once in Account > Wildlife licence, and every handover swaps them, so nobody has to find and send them. Every handover is listed in Account > Sales and purchases.

Photos and notifications

How do I add photos?

On an animal's page, add photos from your library or camera, and choose the cover photo shown on the Animals list. Photos are part of Pro and need a connection to upload.

How do morning notifications work?

With Pro, turn them on in Account: one notification at the hour you choose, with what's due to feed and a heads-up before lays and pairing season. They come to phones with the app installed (not the web app).

Your account and data

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Changes are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online. Adding photos, handovers and the Help contact form need a connection.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your junk or spam folder for an email from Clutchly (no-reply@clutchly.com.au). If it isn't there after a few minutes, contact us below with the email address you signed up with.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Account > Download my data saves a file with all your animals, history, pairings, routine, licence details, handovers and photo list. It works on the free and Pro plans.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the round account button at the top right, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, animals, records and photos (completed handovers stay in the other person's records). If you can't sign in, email support@eternalreptiles.com.au from the email address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Free and Pro

What's the difference between free and Pro?

Free: up to 10 animals (animals marked Sold don't count) with every record-keeping feature and data export. Pro: unlimited animals, photos and morning notifications.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Pro?

iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > your profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Your data is never deleted when Pro ends.
