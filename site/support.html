Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the round account button at the top right, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, animals, records and photos. If you can't sign in, email support@eternalreptiles.com.au from the email address on your account and we'll delete it for you within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Account > Download my data saves a file with all your animals, history, pairings, routine and photo list. It works on the free and Pro plans.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Pro?

iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > your profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Your data is never deleted when Pro ends.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Changes are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online. Adding photos needs a connection.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.
