Know who's due

Overview shows who's due now and the week ahead, day by day: feeds, expected lays and hatches, and your season dates. Refusal streaks are flagged.

Your routine, your way

Feed intervals for males and females per species, a seasonal feeding pause and pairing dates. Starting routines for carpets (including jungles and coastals), Bredli, diamonds, and Children's, Spotted, Stimson's and Pygmy pythons. Feeds pause while an animal is in shed or paired.

Breeding, start to finish

Locks, ovulation and pre-lay shed with an expected date for each next stage. Create hatchlings with your own ID pattern, linked to both parents.

Morph calculator

Record each animal's genes and see every possible baby with its odds, including Sunglow, Moonglow, Snow and Ghost, and health warnings such as Jaguar.

Feed the whole rack

Mark everyone due as fed in one go, change any to refused or not offered, set the food once and save. Every entry stays on each animal's history.

Incubation log

Incubator, target temperature, and readings of temperature, humidity and good eggs. Anything off target stands out.

QR labels

Print labels for tubs on A4 sticker sheets or a label printer. Scan one with your phone to open that animal, or scan two to start a pairing.

Hand over to the buyer

Send a link and, once you approve, your buyer gets the animal with its feeding, shed and weight history, genes and photos. Wildlife licence details are swapped for both of you.

Works offline

Log in the reptile room with no signal. Everything syncs when you're back online, across your phone and the web.

Private by design

Your collection is visible only to you. No ads, no tracking, and you can download everything you've entered at any time.
