## Apple App Store

**Subtitle** (30 max, 30 used): `Reptile feeding & breeding log`

**Promotional text** (170 max, can change any time without review):
Track feeds, sheds, weights, pairings and clutches for your whole collection - even with no signal in the reptile room.

**Keywords** (100 max, 99 used; commas, no spaces; the name and category are already indexed):
`reptile,python,snake,breeding,feeding,shed,clutch,hatchling,gecko,dragon,herp,carpet,lay,weight,log`

**Description** (4000 max):

Clutchly is the record book for reptile keepers and breeders. Log feeds, refusals, sheds and weights in a tap, see
who is due today, and follow every pairing from introduction to hatchlings.

KNOW WHO'S DUE
• Overview shows who is due now and the week ahead day by day: feeds, lays, hatches and season dates
• Flags refusal streaks and lays or hatches that are past their estimated date
• Set feed intervals for males and females per species, plus a seasonal feeding pause
• Morning reminder of what's due (Pro)

EVERY RECORD IN ONE PLACE
• Feeds and refusals with food type, sheds, and weights on any date
• Growth chart, average days between sheds and refusal rate for each animal
• Full history with filters; edit or delete any entry
• Photos of every animal (Pro)

BREEDING, START TO FINISH
• Pairings with locks, ovulation and pre-lay shed
• Estimated lay and hatch dates (estimated birth for live-bearing skinks)
• Morph calculator: record each animal's genes (visual, het, possible het) and see every possible baby with its odds,
  with combination names (Sunglow, Moonglow, Snow, Ghost) and health warnings such as Jaguar
• Create hatchlings with your own ID pattern, linked to both parents and carrying their hets
• Heads-up a week before a lay and three days before pairing season (Pro)

MADE FOR THE REPTILE ROOM
• Works offline: changes sync when you're back in signal
• 34 species, from carpet pythons and Antaresia to dragons, monitors, geckos, skinks and turtles
• Starting routines for carpets, Bredli, diamonds and Antaresia, for southern and northern hemispheres
• Paste a list or import a CSV to add your whole collection at once
• Australian or US date format

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your collection is visible only to you. No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold. Download everything you've
entered at any time, or delete your account from inside the app.

FREE AND PRO
Clutchly is free for up to 10 animals with every record-keeping feature. Clutchly Pro adds unlimited animals, photos
and notifications for A$59.99 a year. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before
the end of the period; manage it in your Apple ID settings. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted.
