# Screenshot plan

Capture these from a release or preview build signed in as the demo account (Australian date format). Before you start,
add 3-4 real photos of your own animals to Ruby, Jag and Pearl in the demo account so the Animals list shows covers.
Hide the status-bar clutter (full battery, good signal, a tidy time like 9:41) and turn on light mode.

## Sizes

| Store | Device | Size (portrait, pixels) | How many |
|---|---|---|---|
| App Store | iPhone 6.9" (e.g. iPhone 16/17 Pro Max) | 1320 × 2868 (1290 × 2796 also accepted) | 3-10 (aim for 6) |
| App Store | iPhone 6.5" (optional; Apple scales the 6.9" set down) | 1284 × 2778 | optional |
| App Store | iPad | not needed: iPad support is off for version 1 (`supportsTablet: false`) | 0 |
| Google Play | Phone | 1080 × 1920 to 1440 × 3120 (9:16 to 9:19.5) | 2-8 (aim for 6) |
| Google Play | Feature graphic | 1024 × 500 (logo and "Clutchly" on cream #fcfcf8) | 1 (required) |
| Google Play | App icon | 512 × 512 (export `assets/icon.png` at 512 px) | 1 (required) |

Use the same six screens in the same order for both stores. A short caption above each screenshot (serif, black on
cream, matching the app) helps; keep it to 2-6 words.

## Order

1. **Today** – caption: *"Know who's due today"*. The Today tab with 5-7 animals due, mix of "due today" and
   "1d overdue". Fed/Refused buttons visible.
2. **Animal page with growth chart** – caption: *"Growth, sheds and refusals"*. Open Ruby, scroll so the Growth chart
   and the two stat cards (days between sheds, refusal rate) fill the screen.
3. **Pairing with estimated lay** – caption: *"Follow every pairing"*. Ruby × Jag: status line "Pre-lay shed ·
   estimated lay …" and the list of locks.
4. **Animals list with photos** – caption: *"Your whole collection"*. Animals tab with cover photos, weights and last
   fed / shed.
5. **Create hatchlings** – caption: *"Hatchlings, named your way"*. Open Pearl × Shadow > Create hatchlings, fill in
   clutch 14, fertile 12, pattern `DEMO-{YYYY}-{##}` so the preview shows the names. Don't tap Create.
6. **Season routine** – caption: *"Your routine, per species"*. Season tab showing the Carpet card with feeding pause,
   pairing start and intervals.

Optional 7th and 8th (Google allows 8): **Bulk add** (paste preview with a few rows) – *"Add your collection in
seconds"*; **Offline** (the "Offline · 3 to sync" pill on Today with airplane mode on) – *"Works with no signal"*.

Don't show: the Pro screen as a main screenshot (Apple allows it, but it reads as an ad), anything with test data
names, or the web version.
